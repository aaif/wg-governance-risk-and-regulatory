# [Report] Governance, Risk & Regulatory Alignment - October 2026

**Working Group:** WG Governance, Risk & Regulatory Alignment

**Reporting period:** October 2026

**Chair(s):** Deborah Eng (JPMorgan Chase) & Sriram Natarajan (Google)

**Date submitted:** 2026-11-22

---

## Summary

In October, the Working Group consolidated regional and framework evaluations across its 16 governance themes into the Phase 2 Interim Report (*Global Regulatory & Standards Gap Analysis for Agentic AI*), now available for review ahead of moving to Git in early December. Following review of the *Operationalizing Agent Governance* proposal, the WG advanced two practitioner deliverables, evolving its logging guidance into the *Operational Telemetry vs. Legal Records: Best Practice Guide* alongside the *Legal Context Schema*. The group also reviewed and provided formal scope-alignment feedback supporting the proposed Attestation & Evidence WG, evaluated the GovOps architecture against charter objectives.

## Progress Against Objectives

- **Phase 2 Gap Analysis Interim Report:**
  - Building on prior WG methodology discussions and standardized theme extraction prompts, the group consolidated regional statutory and agentic framework findings across the 16 baseline themes.
  - The Phase 2 Interim Report, [*Global Regulatory & Standards Gap Analysis for Agentic AI*](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19iYPCbRdlUWKZU_Z4JprguW0ko--B_OfEsdmlAfB654/edit?resourcekey=0-gHox-BXxo8V6NKduPslwjA), is now available for review, and the WG intends to migrate it to Git as part of the final proposal in early December.
- **Operationalizing Agent Governance & Core Deliverables:**
  - Reviewed the [*AAIF WG Proposal: Operationalizing Agent Governance*](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XSLx_sLZyqWp6_SRJ2wLGUMLrqlCHsxkCT6VFimswoQ/edit?resourcekey=0-qIdCTgtMxLEI9XXit1QRqw&tab=t.0#heading=h.cv19ud82eca4), aligning contributors on translating regulatory obligations into actionable lifecycle guidance, runtime legal context, and evidentiary criteria.
  - Evolved the evidence vs. logs workstream (building on converging record-shape discussions in [#9](https://github.com/aaif/wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory/issues/9)) into an active draft of the [*Operational Telemetry vs. Legal Records: Best Practice Guide*](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16CptskJeBNRUL4RXJCs3IZ-_o_UUb5uYfgX6X3c7mdM/edit?tab=t.wlkvy14imsgn&resourcekey=0-8D_qxMz3f3Z5ZmGOskGOwA), and continued advancing updates to the **Legal Context Schema**.
- **Cross-WG & Architecture Alignment:**
  - **Attestation & Evidence WG Proposal:** Reviewed [aaif/working-group-proposals#6](https://github.com/aaif/working-group-proposals/issues/6) as a working group and submitted [formal feedback](https://github.com/aaif/working-group-proposals/issues/6#issuecomment-6812002742) supporting its formation with a clear scope handshake. Under this boundary, AE-WG focuses on technical properties of verifiable records, attestations, witness taxonomies, and record completeness, while this WG retains regulatory interpretation, obligation mapping, and regulatory sufficiency (with a designated GRR PoC to collaborate on shared evidence vocabulary).
  - **GovOps Architecture:** Reviewed the OWASP [GovOps architecture](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Y2tiuPZ_AHTTzs3RX5h5rgY9V32lhd4ABOEjnsCpM_c/edit?slide=id.p#slide=id.p) presentation and reflected on how it aligns with our Working Group's charter; this work will inform practical inputs that can be aligned with regulatory expectations.
- **AGNTCon + MCPCon North America Participation & Debrief:**
  - WG members attended AGNTCon + MCPCon North America in person and debriefed key ecosystem and governance takeaways with the broader working group.
