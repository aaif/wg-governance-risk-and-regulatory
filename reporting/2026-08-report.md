## Summary

<!-- 2–3 sentences capturing the month at a glance. -->
The working group agreed to a set of 16 themes to form the baseline against which the phase two comparative/gap analysis would be conducted/measured. The group also discussed (and finalized) developing a “golden source” AI prompt to ensure information for phase two of the project is extracted consistently across regional policy instruments. Multiple working group members agreed to collaborate on the prompt, including by developing language for identifying a golden-source statement for each theme and potentially conducting comparative/gap analyses across documents. 

## Progress Against Objectives

<!-- What shipped, what advanced. Tie back to your stated objectives where possible. -->

- The group agreed to a set of 16 metrics to use as the baseline against which to assess the gaps in existing AI/agentic AI rules and regulations:
  - **Governance & accountability** (ownership, approvals, RACI, risk acceptance)
  - **Use-case classification & risk tiering** (allowed/prohibited, tiered controls)
  - **Architecture** (reference patterns, trust boundaries, control placement)
  - **Agent identity & delegation** (authN/Z model, acting-on-behalf-of, registry)
  - **Guardrails & policy constraints** (business rules, policy-as-code, constraints)
  - **Data protection & privacy** (PII, minimization, retention, residency)
  - **Security & access controls** (least privilege, secrets, segmentation, tool perms)
  - **Model/agent risk management** (validation, robustness, drift, change control)
  - **Human oversight** (HITL/HOTL, escalation, override/kill switch, automated oversight)
  - **Action management** (pre-execution checks, dual control, reversibility)
  - **Monitoring & logging** (audit trails, prompt/tool logs, decision logs)
  - **Testing & red teaming** (prompt injection, tool abuse, autonomy failures)
  - **Third-party & supply chain** (vendor due diligence, model/tool provenance)
  - **Incident response** (detection, containment, reporting)
  - **Transparency & user disclosure** (notices, explanations, user expectations)
  - **Recordkeeping & auditability** (retention schedules, reproducibility)
- The group also assembled a sample prompt for working group members to use/leverage in conducting their particular geographic analysis against the above baseline.
- The goal for September will be to finalize the second phase (comparative/gap analysis) of the project and decide on an interim report/work product to release to the TC and broader working groups.
