## Summary

<!-- 2–3 sentences capturing the month at a glance. -->
In September, the Working Group advanced Phase 2 of the regulatory gap analysis, standardizing prompt-driven regional policy evaluations against the established 16-theme baseline. To ensure high utility across the broader consortium, the group focused discussions on the deliverable and engagement with other WGs. Following Ryan's departure, leadership transitioned with Deborah Eng stepping in as Chair; an open call for nominations was subsequently conducted across the working group, resulting in Sriram Natarajan being selected as Co-Chair. Together, the chairs are driving group engagement and operational cadence. Furthermore, the WG is evaluating additional deliverables, notably advancing a proposal to operationalize agent governance.
